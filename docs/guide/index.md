# The guide

Interval is three tabs and a gear:

**Today**
:   What to do now. The week, a line on what kind of day it is, and the day's steps in order with one Start.

**Train**
:   Everything you can start, in three pillars: **Strength** (Lift's programs, sessions and exercises),
    **Conditioning** (the presets, with the Timer a page over them) and **Mobility** (the flows). The bar reopens
    the pillar you last used.

**Log**
:   What you did and what's planned: the week by kind of training, the month's calendar, each finished workout
    and session, sport days, and the plan with its reminders.

**Settings** (the gear on every tab)
:   Your weight, sounds and voice, Health Connect or Apple Health, a heart-rate sensor, the plan's reminders,
    and export, import and clearing the log.
