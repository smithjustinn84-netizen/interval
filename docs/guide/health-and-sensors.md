# Health & sensors

## Health Connect and Apple Health

Connect in **Settings**. Each workout, flow and Lift session you finish is then written there as a workout:

- its intervals or sets, and pauses;
- a calorie estimate, from the activity, its length and your body weight;
- how hard you said it was, and your note;
- flows as stretching.

Interval reads two things, each only if you allow it:

**Your weight**
:   The latest from the last 30 days, which Settings' weight sheet offers as a button. It's never taken on its
    own.

**Your heart rate**
:   A watch's average and peak during a workout or session you finished, shown on its summary and its page.

Turn it off in Settings, or take the permission back, and nothing more is written; what was written stays.
On iOS, refusing on Apple Health's sheet sends you to the Health app to change it.

## Live heart rate (Android)

With a Polar sensor paired, a workout shows your heart rate as a scale of the five zones under the ring: the
beats a minute, the zone by name and number, a marker where in it you are, and the heart rates the zone changes
at. A Lift session shows it as a badge in its title bar, and its summary draws the session's heart rate and the
time in each zone. The zones are worked out from the max heart rate set in Settings, the same on both.

A move into high effort or back down to recovery is called with a tone, while **Heart-rate alerts** and **Sound**
are both on in Settings. If the signal is lost, or the strap comes off, the reading says "No signal" (the scale
stays in place unlit with the last reading) and the sensor is tried again until it is back.

## A class on a sensor (Android 12+)

Record a whole class on a **Polar Verity Sense** or a **Polar H10** chest strap with the phone in your bag. One
is paired at a time, and either shows your heart rate live in a workout too:

1. **Pair** it in Settings, under *Heart-rate sensor*. The app asks for leave to reach nearby devices; it never
   asks for your location.
2. On a class day, Today offers **Record class**. The sensor records on its own memory.
3. Afterwards, **Sync class**. It logs the day and opens the class: its length, its heart rate drawn with each
   round shaded, the average and peak, and a row a round:
   *Round 3 · 5:02 · 162 avg · 178 peak · down 24 in 60 s*.

The rounds are found from the heart rate and how much your arm moved. An H10 records the heart rate alone, so
its rounds go by the heart rate. The class's row in the Log opens it again.
Deleting the recording leaves the day logged.
