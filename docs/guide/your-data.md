# Your data

Everything is kept on your device, in the app's own storage. There's no account and nothing to sign in to. See
the [privacy policy](../privacy.md).

## Export and import

**Settings › Export** puts everything in one JSON file: workouts, sport days, presets and sequences, the plan,
settings, Lift's sessions, programs, exercises and gear, your own flows, and recorded classes. Share it wherever
you keep files.

**Import** reads a file from any version of the app, shows what it holds before anything changes, skips what's
already there, and checks every value is in range.

**Clear the log** is there too, if you want to start over.

## Backup

On Android, the system's backup carries the app's data to a new phone with your other apps'.

## Widgets and shortcuts

**Android**
:   A home-screen widget with the week's goal ring, your streak and one-tap starts. Long-press the app's icon for
    *Start a workout* and *Start a Lift session*.

**iOS**
:   Home Screen and Lock Screen widgets with the week. Long-press the icon for the same quick actions. A workout
    or a Lift timer running shows as a Live Activity.

## Accessibility

Headings and every control are named for TalkBack and VoiceOver, and looping motion stops when the system asks
for reduced motion.
